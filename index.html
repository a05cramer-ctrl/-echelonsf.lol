How it works

The numbers come straight from chain, not from anyone's screenshots.

01 · READ

Every StonkFun trade

Every swap on every active StonkFun coin, read from Solana: who traded, which coin, how much STONK in or out.

02 · SCORE

Profit, not volume

Per wallet over 7 days: what they sold for, minus what they paid, plus what they still hold at today's price. Bots and pool accounts are left out.

03 · COPY

One click, your wallet

When a top wallet buys, you can buy the same coin through Jupiter at the size you picked. Your wallet signs. ECHELON never holds anything.

Proof: every 10 minutes the board is published to IPFS and its address is written on chain in a memo (ECHELON-BOARD) from the ECHELON wallet , so anyone can check what was ranked and when.
